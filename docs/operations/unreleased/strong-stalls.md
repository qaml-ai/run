### camelrun/strong: stalls and rate limits are found in seconds, and the pair fails fast

- **First token:** `camelrun/strong`'s members (Gemini 4 Carbon on flex, then on the default tier) wait for a first token for 30 seconds plus 0.4 seconds per thousand input tokens, at most the runtime's usual wait. This replaces 120 seconds, or 300 when thinking hard. Measured on OpenRouter on 2026-11-12, the first token came in 5–31 s up to 60k tokens and 63–71 s at 150k. A member that sends nothing by then has stalled, and the other member takes the request at once (no flex wait after a stall). An agent's own `runLimits.firstTokenSeconds` still wins. Other models are unchanged.
- **Flex wait:** at most 10 seconds (`STRONG_FLEX_WAIT_CAP_SECONDS`), down from 30 by default on the Workers backend; the Node runtime, which moved at once, now waits the same. Flex answered a 60k-token request with an immediate 429 in 10 of 10 probes, before and after our own Google key on OpenRouter.
- **Both members down:** a 429 or 5xx from the default tier after flex fails the request at once. Its error starts with the default tier's own, then names both members' errors ("…no member can take this now; …; it is retried after a backoff"). Pi's retry backs off and starts again at flex, so the backoff is the pair's.
