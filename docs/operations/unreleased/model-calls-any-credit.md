### A model call starts on any end-user credit left

Following "A person can spend the credit they have": a model call no longer needs its estimate within $0.25 of what is
left. Any credit starts it, and it holds what is left; it gets at least 256 tokens of output, and what it costs past its
hold is written off. Fixed-price work keeps the $0.25. A refusal no longer says `$0.00 is left`.
