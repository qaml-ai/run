### Prepaid account balances can go negative

A model call on the platform's key can start with any positive spendable credit. It can finish at its full output budget even when its cost exceeds the remaining account credit. The account pays the full metered cost, and new calls are refused once spendable credit is zero or negative. Free hourly allowances, dispute restrictions and existing spend limits still apply. Concurrent calls can start against the same positive balance, within the existing busy-agent and run-concurrency limits.
