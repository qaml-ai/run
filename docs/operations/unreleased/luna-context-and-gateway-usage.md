Luna models use their native 272,000-token context window across OpenRouter, Azure, and other aggregators, including Pro and routing variants and agents created before this correction. Other models keep their Pi catalog window. Learned token limits are stored in a small document, survive restarts, and cannot fall below 32,768 tokens or half the catalog window. Byte-size refusals do not lower token limits. Overflow recovery retracts refused responses and respects cancellation and host handoff.

Failed model calls without usage evidence are never billed. Unreconcilable calls settle at zero when no usage was reported; platform Azure and ordinary tenant provider endpoints are not classified as gateways. Reconciliation waits past the maximum turn time, preserves the credentials recorded for the attempt, and never increases an end user's charge after the call. Calls still streaming refresh their activity time. Direct OpenRouter credentials continue to reconcile against the provider's generation record.
