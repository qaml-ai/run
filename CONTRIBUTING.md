# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve the camelAI agent runtime.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before we can merge your first pull request, you need to sign our
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA check on your pull request tells you how:
comment the sentence it asks for, once. It covers all your future contributions too.

The CLA lets us offer the runtime both under the AGPL-3.0 and under commercial licenses.
You keep the copyright in your work.

## Licenses

See [License](README.md#license) in the README: the runtime is AGPL-3.0-only, while the
SDKs (`clients/`, `sdk/`), the frontend packages (`packages/`) and `examples/` are MIT.
Contributions to each part are released under that part's license.

Only add production dependencies under permissive licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC and
similar). Copyleft licenses such as GPL, AGPL, SSPL or BUSL can't be used.
